# 01 The cue engine: one-shot voices, pulses cut with a fade, more voices

Status: resolved (2026-11-13)
Type: task
Blocked by: none

Spec: `.scratch/items/spec.md` ("The cue engine"). Read it first, then `docs/a11y/cues.md` ("Mental model", "The engine's rules", "The once voice") and ADR-0005.

## What to build

- **A one-shot voice.** A new voice mode for cues: created with a sound, it plays that sound once from the start each time it is triggered, with pan, pitch and gain targets, through its cue's level, so Accessibility, Cues, the cue volume and the cue's own switch, volume and boost reach it as they reach every cue voice. A trigger while it still plays restarts it from the top, the old play fading out as for a cut pulse (below). It goes silent by itself at the sound's end. It ramps in and out like every voice. The speech voices' once mode stays as it is: it ignores pitch and the cue levels on purpose.
- **A pool for bursts.** A way for a cue to own several one-shot voices and trigger the next free one, taking the oldest playing when none is free, so several items appearing on one tick each sound. The pool's size is a constant.
- **Pulses cut with a fade.** A pulsed voice whose sound is longer than its fastest interval at its lowest pitch is no longer refused. When the next pulse is due while the last still plays, the old one fades out over a few milliseconds as the new one starts from the top. The slow pace plays a long sound whole; a fast one hears its start. Sounds that already fit behave exactly as before, so the existing cues are unchanged.
- **More room.** Raise `kMaxVoices` and `kMaxCues` to hold the two item cues, the appearance cue's pool, their preview twins, and a few tracked items later.

## Done when

## Comments

### 2026-11-13, implementation (agent)

Implemented; the engine has one-shot voices with a pool for overlapping plays, cuts a long pulse with a fade instead of refusing it, and holds 32 voices and 32 cues.

- **Where things live.** All in `cue_engine.hpp/.cpp`, so `a11y.cmake` is unchanged. `VoiceMode::one_shot`; `CueEngine::trigger(voice, targets)` plays it; `OneShotPool` beside the engine. The once voice is untouched.
- **The one-shot.** Added with its sound like any cue voice (refused without one). Each `trigger` writes the targets and counts a trigger; the audio thread takes the count, starts the play from the top at those targets (no glide from the last play's pan or pitch) and ramps it in over the start ramp; it ramps out over the sound's last frames and goes silent by itself, as the once voice does. `set_targets` while it plays glides it. Its chain is a cue voice's: master × mix level × its cue's level × its gain target (held to 1), so Accessibility, Cues, Cue volume and the cue's switch, volume and boost reach it, and `ignores_switches` works for a preview twin. `start` does nothing to it. `stop` ramps the play out and drops a trigger the audio thread has not begun (a trigger then a stop on one tick plays nothing). `playing` is the audio thread's word, true from `trigger` until the play has ended or ramped out, with the same taken-then-busy ordering as the once voice. Each trigger logs `[a11y] cue <name> play pan .. pitch .. gain ..`; stops are not logged. `trigger` on any other voice logs `cue voice <n> cannot be triggered` and returns false.
- **The cut with a fade.** One mechanism for both: a voice keeps one `Tail`, a play cut by the next, which goes on from where it was at its own pan, pitch and gain, fading from its level to 0 over the stop ramp (5 ms), while the new play starts at position 0. A pulse is cut only when its position is still inside the sound when the next is due; a sound that fits its fastest interval at its lowest pitch has always played out by then, so no tail is ever made for it and the existing cues render sample for sample as before (the old test of onsets, the pulse train dump and a new exact-fit test check it). A pulsed voice's tail also takes the voice's start and stop ramp, so a stop silences both. The refusal and its log line are gone; `fastest_interval` and `lowest_pitch` still hold the targets.
- **The pool.** `OneShotPool::kSize` is 4: a Party Ball or a burst of container contents rarely brings more at once, and a fifth takes the oldest. `add(engine, spec)` adds `kSize` one-shots from one spec, named `<name> 1` to `<name> 4`; when the engine runs out of voices it returns false and keeps those it got. `trigger(engine, targets)` takes the first voice not playing, else the one triggered longest ago (kept by a trigger count in the pool, game thread only), and returns the voice, or -1 for an empty pool. `stop`, `playing` and `voices()` (a span, for the trace or a silence by name) cover them all.
- **Limits.** `kMaxVoices` and `kMaxCues` 32. Voices in use today 12; the items feature as planned adds 4 + 4 (the appearance pool and its preview twin) and 1 + 1 (the item's position cue and its twin), 22, leaving room for several tracked items with their twins. Cues in use 4, 6 with the item cues. Nothing else sizes by either constant.
- **Tests** (`test_cue_engine.cpp`): a one-shot at a pan and a pitch played once and silent after (an octave up half as long, full left nothing on the right), with no step past a ramp's; its cue's volume, boost, its gain held to 1, its cue's switch and the mix's silencing it while it plays on, and a preview's past both switches; a retrigger mid-play with no click, the whole sound again from the retrigger at the new gain, and a retrigger from full left to full right fading on the left as it ramps in on the right; a stop, and a trigger stopped before it was taken; a pool's burst on four distinct voices sounding together, the two next triggers taking the oldest in order, a free voice taken before a playing one, a stop of all, and a pool that only half fits the engine; a 0.3 s pulse at a 0.1 s pace cut at full level, silent from the fade's end through the next pulse's lead-in, no click, and at a 0.5 s pace played whole; a pulse that fits exactly rendering at a constant level, no tail added. A mutation (the cut's fade set to 0) fails the long pulse test on the click.
- **Verification**, quick and online levels with `MELEE_A11Y_CUES=1`: build with the "Rollback sections" line; style; unit tests, 38 of 38 (`launcher_data` passed this time); the seven match scripts, 7 of 7, every match cue starting as before and no `refused` line in any log; `run_level.py online`, 9 of 9, taken since the change is to the cues, though the engine never reaches game state.
- **Docs.** `docs/a11y/cues.md`: the mental model's modes and diagram, "A long pulse is cut with a fade" in place of "A pulse must fit", and a new section "The one-shot voice" with "The pool", which says how a driver uses it; a sentence about game sounds no longer calls them one-shots. ADR-0005 unchanged: the decision did not move. The `verify` skill's `cue_engine` suite description. CLAUDE.md's cue engine status line names the one-shot voices, the pool and the cut.
- **For issue 02.** A one-shot ramps in at each trigger, so a file's attack needs the engine's lead-in before it, as a pulse's does (`lead_in` in `cue_sounds.hpp`); the end ramp makes a file that stops short harmless but eats its last 5 ms. The fastest interval at which a pulse plays whole, for the script's report, is the sound's length (lead-in included) divided by the cue's lowest pitch.
- **For issue 04.** Keep one `OneShotPool` for the appearance cue and a second made from the same spec with `ignores_switches` for its preview; per new item call `pool.trigger(engine, targets)` with the item's pan and pitch, nothing more. `MatchCues::silence` (the port menu and scene entry) must call `pool.stop(engine)` for the match's pool, not the preview's; `stop_all` would reach both. A trigger is logged per voice (`cue item appearance 2 play ...`), which the drive scripts can count.
- **For issue 05.** A pulsed voice with a sound longer than its fast interval is now taken; set `fastest_interval` to the within-reach pace and `lowest_pitch` to the bottom of the pitch range as before, since they still clamp the targets. Moving the voice to another item is `set_targets`, which keeps the rhythm; only a stop and a start from silence restart it.
- **Left open.** The pool's size and the cut's fade length (the stop ramp's 5 ms) are first proposals for the play test.
