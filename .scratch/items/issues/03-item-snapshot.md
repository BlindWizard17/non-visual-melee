# 03 The item snapshot and the container names

Status: resolved (2026-11-13)
Type: task
Blocked by: none

Spec: `.scratch/items/spec.md` ("What the game gives us", "Which items", "The snapshot", open questions 1, 2, 3 and 5). Read it first, then `docs/a11y/gameplay-state.md` ("Reading safely", "Items") and `docs/a11y/cues.md` ("The match snapshot").

## What to build

- **The items in the match snapshot.** `a11y_game_match_state` (`game_access.c`) walks the item list once a tick and keeps a short list of items in the fork's sense: of a common kind and not made by a fighter (`flags.x1`). Each holds its serial (`x1C`), kind, position, whether it is loose, whether it is within reach of you, and who holds it (a slot, or none). The list is ordered nearest to you first, by straight-line distance, and its length is a constant, long enough to find a Party Ball's whole burst. The snapshot also says how many items there were, so a reader knows when the list was cut short.
- **Loose**: grabbable (`Item_IsGrabbable`), not held, and not flying after a throw; one thrown is loose again once it lands (open question 2 settles the field).
- **Within reach**: `ftpickupitem_800942A0(your fighter, 3)` returns the item; mark that one. Called from the snapshot only, between ticks, as the floor cast is; it only reads (the spec, "Within reach"). Not called while you are out.
- **Taking, for issue 06.** Per slot, what the spec's open question 1 finds is needed to see a take: the items in hand (`item_gobj`, `x1978`, `x197C`) by serial, and whatever marks a touch item taken and by whom. Find the answer by probing and put it in the snapshot.
- **Names.** `item_kinds.h` and `item_names.cpp` gain Capsule, Crate, Barrel and Egg (`It_Kind_Capsule`, `It_Kind_Box`, `It_Kind_Taru`, `It_Kind_Egg` in `forward.h`), keeping the static asserts whole; Item Switch and the Training menu are unaffected.
- **The open questions.** Answer 1, 2, 3 and 5 by probing in Training (its item menu brings any item in beside you; `tools/a11y/training_menu.drive` reaches it) and in a VS match with items on if one can be had (question 4 is issue 07's, but note what you learn). Temporary logging is fine; remove it before committing.

## Comments

### 2026-11-13, implementation (agent)

Implemented; the match snapshot lists the items nearest to you first, the match HUD snapshot gives each slot its hands and its tally of items taken, and every common item has a name.

- **The item list** (`A11yMatchState`, `game_access.h`): `items[A11Y_MATCH_ITEMS]` with `item_count` listed and `items_in_match` all of them, more only when the list was cut. `A11Y_MATCH_ITEMS` is 32: a Party Ball drops up to 15 on one tick (`itkusudama.c`), while the game's caps are 40 common items and 100 Food at once (`Item_804A0C64`, measured), so only several Party Balls of Food could cut it. Each `A11yMatchItem` has `serial` (`x1C`), `kind` (`A11yItem`), `x`, `y` (`pos`), `airborne`, `loose`, `within_reach` and `holder` (a slot, -1 none). Ordered by straight-line distance from your feet, an insertion into the fixed array as the item list is walked. Filled whenever `running` is, paused ticks included; `within_reach` only while you are not out.
- **An item** is of a kind below `It_Kind_L_Gun_Ray` with `xDC8_word.flags.x1` set. The flag alone is not enough: a stage hazard's hit area (kind 0xA0) had it set in Princess Peach's Castle.
- **Loose** is `(touch kind || Item_IsGrabbable) && !x13 && !x14`. Deviation from the issue's wording: the Starman and the Mushrooms are never grabbable (no pickup callback; the probe showed `Item_IsGrabbable` false all their lives), so they are loose by kind; otherwise the item's position cue could never follow them, which the spec means it to (touch items are "never within reach", so they are followed).
- **Holder**: `x13` set, the holder `owner` (or `x51C` for an item with `flags.x0`, as `it_802742F4` keeps it), found on the live fighter list and named by its `player_idx`, Nana included.
- **Within reach**: `ftpickupitem_800942A0(your entity, 3)`, called from the snapshot between ticks; it walks the item list and reads only (`it_8026B344`, `itIsHeavy`, `it_8026B47C`, `it_8026B4F0`, `itGetGrabRange*`, all field reads). The item it returns gets the mark: at most one, and none if its answer is no item of the fork's (a turnip, say).
- **Taking** (`A11yMatchHudPlayer`, filled by `a11y_game_match_hud`, not `a11y_game_match_state`: issue 06's announcer already reads the HUD snapshot per slot with its labels and panels, and the take data is per slot too). `hands[A11Y_MATCH_HANDS]`, the main fighter's `item_gobj`, `x1978`, `x197C`, each an `A11yMatchHeld` `{serial, kind}`, serial -1 for an empty hand or anything not an item of the fork's; each pointer followed only once found on the live item list. `taken[A11Y_ITEM_COMMON_KINDS]`, the game's tally by kind, `pl_8003E2CC(slot, kind)`. As before, nothing is filled for a slot that is not `present`, so its hands read serial 0 from the memset: check `present` first.
- **Names**: `item_kinds.h` now mirrors all 35 common kinds (the four containers added, checked by the static asserts like the rest) and gains `A11Y_ITEM_COMMON_KINDS`, asserted against `It_Kind_L_Gun_Ray` and against the tally's size; `item_names.cpp` names Capsule, Crate, Barrel and Egg. Item Switch and the Training menu keep their own tables of kinds, so they are unchanged. Test `item_names_name_every_common_item` in `test_training_speech.cpp`, which links `item_names.cpp`, written first and seen failing.
- **No C++ seam**: the ordering and loose live in `game_access.c` beside the walk, so there is no pure logic outside it to unit-test; the Training probe checked them.

**Open question 1, seeing a take.** Comparing the hands alone misses touches and the air catch (Z) of a healing item, Metal Box or Cloaking Device, which `fn_800D6F58` uses up in the same call; the tally alone misses a re-pickup (it counts a slot's first hold only, by the item's `xF` bit). Together they catch every take. The rule for issue 06, per present slot each tick from GO:
1. Hand takes: each serial in this tick's `hands` (taken as a set of serials) not in the slot's set last tick, with its kind from `hands`. The Bunny Hood moving from `item_gobj` to `x197C`, and Zelda's and Sheik's change, which hands the held item to the other form, keep the serial in the set: no take.
2. Tally takes: for each kind, `taken[kind] - last[kind]` minus the hand takes of that kind this tick; if positive, that many takes more of that kind. A fall (rollback) counts nothing.
The tally rises on the same tick as the hand (both in `ftpickupitem_800948A8` and `fn_800D6F58`), and for a touch on the tick the item leaves the list. It names the slot that touched; the item keeps no field for it, and `flags.xF` is only four bits and never cleared, so it is no help. It also counts a Metal Box broken by an attack (`itMetalB_Logic32_DmgReceived`), which applies the metal as a pickup does: it would be said as a take, which seems right. Kept per slot in issue 06's reader, reset at each match's start (`pc_a11y_scene_entered`), the first tick seen setting the baseline.

**Open question 2, thrown and landed.** `x14` (thrown): set by a throw or drop, cleared by any state change (`Item_80268E5C`). A Ray Gun thrown upwards bounced twice (`xD50_landNum` 1 then 2, still airborne by `ground_or_air`, `x14` still set) and only came to rest 35 ticks after its first landing, `x14` clearing as it entered its ground state; a CPU's thrown Super Scope did the same. A Ray Gun thrown on Kongo Jungle bounced off a wall with `x14` kept. So `x14` alone, which the loose test uses; `landNum` counts bounces and `ground_or_air` flickers during them. A thrown item is grabbable in flight (catchable) and was within reach while flying in front of Pichu. A thrown item can also break as it lands (one did), or fall off the stage.

**Open question 3, the appearing tick.** The first tick on the list is where the item came in, near enough for any ear. An item switch spawn (a Capsule and a Super Scope on Fourside) had already had one tick of gravity, `vel.y` -0.07 and 0.07 below its spawn point, since it is made before the items' updates run; Training's spawn sat exactly at your feet on its first tick and rose 2.4 units onto the ground's surface on the next. Party Ball and container contents were not probed.

**Open question 5, Training's spawn.** A on Item makes the item at once, while the menu holds the match: it is on the list in the paused tick of the press. A Training pause holds the fighters but not the items: an item made then settles, and a Super Mushroom walked away and off the stage while the menu stayed open. So issue 04's appearance cue plays while the menu is open, where the cues are otherwise silent; its announcement queues behind the menu's speech, as the spec has it.

**For issue 07, question 4.** A VS match started from character select with the `--no-card` save's default rules has items on: a Capsule fell after about 12 seconds on Fourside, a Super Scope after 21, and the CPU picked the Scope up and threw it. So `sss_steps.drive`-style scripts reach items without the rules screens; Item Switch can narrow the kinds. The debug match cannot: `onEnterDebugVs` sets `item_freq` to -1 and no environment variable changes it, so a debug match with items needs a base port change (an env beside `MELEE_DEBUG_VS_STOCKS`) or the character select route.
